Gamified quiz app

Quizzes that play like a game: XP and levels, daily streaks, 32 badges, friends and quiz leaderboards, and a 20-second timer on every question, in English or Tagalog. Shipped as an Android APK for a school beta test and showcased at TechCase.

BansaRide

Ride and fare guide for Bansalan

Shows where to catch a tricycle, pedicab, bus or van in Bansalan, what the ride costs for students, seniors, PWD and adults, and where the drivers' groups wait. About 96 real road routes on a map that keeps working without signal, plus rider-reported fares and ride alerts.

GABAY

Virtual campus navigation using augmented reality

An AR wayfinding app for St. Mary's College of Bansalan. Floating signs for each campus location bob and fade with distance, a 3D pin marks the destination, and a "Where to?" search lists every place on campus. Onboarding and UI designed in Figma.

  • Unity
  • C#
  • MultiSet SDK
  • Supabase

PublishedJune 2025

ICT Integration & Student Performance

Asian Journal of Education and Social Studies · Vol. 51, Issue 6

A correlational study of how ICT integration in teaching and learning relates to the perceived academic performance of college students in Bansalan. First-listed co-author, with Alexis Dale Laborte and Karen N. Bangcas.
