BansaRide

Ride and fare guide for Bansalan

Shows where to catch a tricycle, pedicab, bus or van in Bansalan, what the ride costs for students, seniors, PWD and adults, and where the drivers' groups wait. About 96 real road routes on a map that keeps working without signal, plus rider-reported fares and ride alerts.

CapstoneMobile · AR · Real screens

GABAY

Find your way, instantly. AR campus navigation for SMCBI

Pick who you are (personnel, student or visitor), search "Where to?" across offices like the Registrar, Clinic, Guidance Office and every program office, and see how far each one is. After a quick scan of the hallway, floating AR signs and a blue path guide you there, turn by turn.

  • C#
  • MultiSet SDK
  • Supabase
  • Figma
